About SafeRent

Smart Rental Property & Tenant Management Platform designed for safety, transparency, and convenience.

Our Mission

SafeRent is built to revolutionize rental property management by bridging the gap between tenants and property owners. From initial property discovery and digital lease agreements to monthly rent tracking, utility bill management, and maintenance request workflows — SafeRent provides a single, unified digital hub.

Primary Goal
SDG 11 – Sustainable Cities

Promoting sustainable, inclusive, and accessible rental housing options in urban communities. SafeRent empowers tenants to easily find safe housing with transparent pricing and verified property details.

Supporting Goal
SDG 16 – Peace & Strong Institutions

Building trust and transparency between property owners and tenants through clear rental contracts, recorded payment histories, structured maintenance reporting, and fair security deposit settlements.
